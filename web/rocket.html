Intake CFD · potential flow

Axisymmetric finite volumes. Slip walls; incompressible, inviscid, irrotational air. Excludes shocks, turbulence, heat transfer and machinery. Field shown is a computed pressure variation, not pressure loss.

Centrebody support · beam FEA

0.1 N transverse tip load, clamped at the guide entry; assumed isotropic E = 2 GPa. Beam idealization excludes contact, print anisotropy and local stress concentrations.
